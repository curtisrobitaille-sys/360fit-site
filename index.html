The 360fit Story

Built on Real Science & Real Life Experience

Founded in 2007 in Dartmouth, Nova Scotia, 360fit was built on a simple premise: fitness should make your life better, not miserable.

Devin Sherrington (former Personal Trainer and Group Fitness Instructor) holds a background from St. Francis Xavier University and has spent nearly two decades refining fat loss coaching for busy parents, professionals, and adults who don't have hours to spend in a gym every day.

High-Intensity Workouts: Maximize calorie burn in 45 minutes or less.
Flexible Nutrition: Enjoy meals with family and friends without guilt.
Unmatched Community: Join hundreds of Dartmouth locals supporting each other.
